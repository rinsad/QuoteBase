# QuoteBase Quote Generation Video Script

Target length: 2-3 minutes
Tone: professional, clear, client-facing
Format: narrated product/process walkthrough

## Voiceover Script

QuoteBase replaces spreadsheet-driven material quoting with a structured, repeatable workflow.

The process starts with the estimator selecting the customer, job site, material, and quantity. QuoteBase verifies that the job site belongs to the selected customer, then loads the organization's active pricing configuration.

That configuration includes the R1 through R4 material markup ranges, trucking rates, minimums, per-load fees, credit-card surcharge settings, and overhead per ton. These values are maintained as business configuration rather than hardcoded formulas.

Next, QuoteBase evaluates supplier and plant options. The system does not simply choose the lowest raw material cost. It compares the complete delivered result, including material cost, route distance, deadhead distance, truck capacity, load count, fees, and sales tax.

The recommendation logic adapts to the size of the quote. For one load or less, QuoteBase prioritizes the lowest delivered total. For quotes up to three loads, it weighs material and trucking economics together. For larger orders, material economics become the first priority, with total cost and route distance used as tie-breakers.

Once the plant and material option are selected, QuoteBase calculates the material price. The supplier buy cost is combined with the configured markup for the material tier. For ton-based materials, overhead per ton is included. The system also enforces the configured material minimum when needed.

Then QuoteBase calculates trucking. It chooses the appropriate vehicle capacity, determines the number of loads, applies the selected trucking rate, and factors in round-trip travel and deadhead time where available. A trucking minimum is applied when the calculated value is below the configured threshold.

After material and trucking are calculated, the system adds per-load fuel and environmental fees. If the payment terms require a credit-card surcharge, that surcharge is added as well.

Sales tax is resolved from the selected tax rate or from the job-site city, county, and state. Tax is applied to the material, trucking, and fee subtotal to produce the final quote total.

When the estimator saves the quote, QuoteBase creates a draft quote with a full breakdown of material, trucking, fees, tax, and total. It also records important decision details, including the selected supplier, plant recommendation reason, route information, and any manual overrides.

From there, the quote moves through the approval workflow. If approval is enabled, the draft is submitted for admin review. Admins can approve, reject, or request changes, and every status transition is written to the audit log.

Once approved, QuoteBase can generate a branded customer PDF, create a secure quote link, attach the PDF to an email, and send it to the customer. After delivery, the quote can be tracked through viewed, accepted, declined, or changes-requested statuses.

In short, QuoteBase turns quoting into a controlled business process. It recommends the right supplier option, applies the organization's pricing rules, calculates delivered cost, captures approvals, and produces a consistent customer-facing quote.

The result is faster quoting, better traceability, and less dependence on manual spreadsheet maintenance.
